# WP Product Positioning

**Status:** Approved working direction  
**Date:** 3 August 2026  
**Scope:** Product positioning foundation for the VNG GTM playbook. This document does not define campaign channels, rollout tactics, pricing, or implementation.

## Positioning decisions

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Category | VNG's governed enterprise AI platform |
| Primary audience | VNG business-unit and function leaders |
| Business problem | Turn people, workflows, and internal data into measurable AI-enabled work without losing control |
| Primary alternative | Fragmented work across documents, systems, data teams, and disconnected AI tools |
| Lead differentiation | Task-first codification of VNG work into reusable AI Skills and Teammates |
| Lead outcome | Scale each department's best ways of working |
| Creation model | Department experts contribute the working method; the WP team helps codify, test, and publish it |
| Supporting foundation | Governed access to VNG data and systems, using VNG-hosted AI by default |

## Positioning statement

For VNG business and function leaders who need to scale expert practice across their departments, WP is VNG's governed enterprise AI platform that turns proven ways of working into reusable AI Skills and Teammates. Unlike fragmented documents, systems, and disconnected AI tools, WP combines task-first workflows with governed VNG data and VNG-hosted AI, helping more employees deliver consistent, high-quality work.

## Core expression

> **Scale VNG's best ways of working.**

## Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** Scale the department's best ways of working.
2. **Mechanism:** Department experts and the WP team codify those methods into reusable Skills and Teammates.
3. **Employee experience:** Start with a real job and produce useful work, rather than starting with a blank chat.
4. **Trust foundation:** Use governed access to VNG systems and data, with VNG-hosted AI by default.
5. **Proof:** A person outside the WP team uses the workflow repeatedly, keeps the output, completes it without coaching, and produces a measurable cost per completed task.

## Audience emphasis

### Business-unit and function leaders

Lead with scaling expert practice, repeatability, output quality, and measurable task outcomes. Governance supports the promise; it is not the opening message.

### Employees

Lead with Skills and Teammates built for real VNG jobs. Emphasize starting from a task, using relevant context, and producing work they can keep.

### TSE, security, and platform owners

Lead with governed access, delegated entitlements, VNG-hosted inference, visible failure states, and centrally controlled capability boundaries.

## Supporting messages

- **Built around work, not chat:** WP begins with jobs and artifacts, not an empty prompt box.
- **Built with departments, not merely delivered to them:** Department experts own the working method; WP turns it into a reusable product capability.
- **Connected to VNG context:** Skills and Teammates can use approved VNG systems and data while preserving the user's entitlements.
- **Operated within VNG's control boundary:** VNG-hosted AI is the default; external processing is an explicit exception, not an automatic fallback.

## Positioning boundaries

Do not position WP as:

- A VNG copy of ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft Copilot.
- A claim that WP's models are better than frontier models.
- A generic chatbot or an app marketplace measured by connector count.
- A central team replacing departmental expertise.
- A governance product whose main value is restriction or compliance.
- An autonomous-agent platform that can act without human control.

## Evidence required for GTM claims

Every featured Skill or Teammate should demonstrate:

1. A named departmental expert or owner.
2. A specific recurring job and intended user group.
3. A useful artifact or completed work outcome.
4. Repeated use by someone outside the WP team.
5. Completion without live coaching from the builder.
6. Known cost per completed task.
7. Clear data sources, permissions, and output coverage.

## Implication for the GTM playbook

The GTM motion should sell a departmental work outcome, then co-codify the first Skill or Teammate with an expert. It should not lead with software installation, model access, connector count, or a broad invitation to experiment with AI.
